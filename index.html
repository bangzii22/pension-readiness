Final balance target means how much of the starting pension fund should remain at life expectancy. Use 0% to spend down the fund, 25% to keep 25%, or any percentage up to 99%.

How the readiness check works

  1. Subtract mortgage and other debt from current savings to estimate net investable assets.
  2. Project assets to pension age using current savings, monthly saving, and expected return.
  3. Inflate current annual net expenses to pension age using the selected country inflation assumption.
  4. Estimate money needed at pension age by treating the target pension fund as invested during retirement, paying yearly expenses, and preserving the selected final balance percentage.
  5. If there is a gap, calculate extra monthly saving needed and test later pension ages until the gap closes or life expectancy is reached.

This is an educational planning tool, not financial advice. Replace defaults with current official inflation records and personal assumptions before making decisions.
